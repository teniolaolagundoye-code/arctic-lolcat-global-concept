Fishery & Aquaculture Division

Sustainable catfish and tilapia farming, fingerling hatcheries, and aquaculture equipment supply.

Integrated Aquaculture & Input Supply

Headed by certified Fishery and Aquaculture specialists, Arctic-Lolcat Global Concept Limited manages intensive fish farming ponds and supplies high-grade aquaculture inputs across Nigeria—including projects executed for the Lagos State Ministry of Agriculture, IFAD/FGN/LIFE-ND, and L-PRES.

We breed fast-growing African catfish fingerlings and table-size fish in monitored water environments, alongside supplying modern fish processing equipment.

  • Healthy catfish fingerlings, juveniles, and table-size harvest fish.
  • High-protein floating and sinking fish feeds.
  • Modern fish smoking kilns, mini-freezers, and solar water pumping systems.
