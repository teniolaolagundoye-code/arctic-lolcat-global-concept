Animal Science & Husbandry Division

Ethical rearing of cattle, goats, sheep, and pigs backed by veterinary science and modern livestock infrastructure.

Livestock Rearing & Resilience Support

Our Animal Science and Husbandry division raises healthy cattle, goats, rams, and piglets under strict veterinary supervision and balanced nutrition protocols.

Beyond livestock production, A.G.C.L actively supports national livestock productivity through grass fodder supply, specialized animal feed formulation, and the construction of modern hygienic slaughterhouses (such as our L-PRES abattoir projects in Kogi State).

  • Healthy beef cattle, goats, sheep, and piglets for commercial & festive markets.
  • Nutritious grass fodder, formulated livestock feeds, and veterinary medicine.
  • Organic farmyard manure supporting sustainable soil fertility.
