Poultry Farming Division

Delivering healthy broilers, fresh table eggs, and formulated poultry feeds under strict veterinary care.

Commercial Poultry & Feed Formulation

Under our Animal Science & Husbandry division, Arctic-Lolcat Global Concept Limited operates modern poultry production units supported by in-house food science and feed formulation specialists.

We supply commercial poultry birds, farm-fresh eggs, veterinary medications, and balanced poultry feeds to households, retail distributors, and institutional agricultural empowerment programs.

  • Fresh table eggs graded and packaged in wholesale crates.
  • Healthy live and hygienically dressed broiler chickens.
  • Custom-formulated poultry feeds and veterinary medicines.
  • Organic poultry manure recycled to enrich our arable crop farms.
