About Arctic-Lolcat Global Concept Limited (A.G.C.L)

Dynamic, Indigenous & Fully Integrated Agribusiness & Engineering Enterprise

Corporate Introduction & History

Arctic-Lolcat Global Concept Limited has been active in the agribusiness and construction sectors since 2009, specializing in agricultural produce enhancement, livestock husbandry, aquaculture, and agro-infrastructure development. The company was formally incorporated on 20th November, 2017 by the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of the Federal Republic of Nigeria under Registration Number RC 1454346.

Our principal aim is to improve agricultural yield, supply quality farming equipment and inputs to farmers, and elevate the value chain of Nigerian agriculture. Guided by the principles of simplicity, clarity, and efficiency, our operations are powered by qualified agricultural scientists, veterinary experts, engineers, and modern computer-assisted management tools.

In addition to direct farm production, A.G.C.L partners with major national agricultural initiatives—including IFAD/FGN/LIFE-ND and the Livestock Productivity and Resilience Support Project (L-PRES)—to supply inputs, solar irrigation systems, and modern agro-facilities across Nigeria.

Our Vision Statement

To be an acutely-distinct agricultural and agro-allied outfit renowned across the industry for responsive, innovative, and quality service delivery at optimum time, cost, and standard.

Our Mission Statement

Backed by qualified Agriculturists, Animal Scientists, Engineers, and Surveyors, our mission is to deliver cost-effective, sustainable agricultural solutions and become a household name in improving national food production.

Our Pledge

We pledge total commitment to every client and partner—turning every agricultural challenge into a stepping stone to deliver high-quality workmanship, reliable farm produce, and lasting value.
