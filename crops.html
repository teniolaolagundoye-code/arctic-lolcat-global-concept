Crop Production & Agronomy Division

Arable crop cultivation, improved seedlings, solar-powered irrigation, and agro-processing machinery.

Modern Agronomy & Mechanized Farming

Led by our Crop Science and Agronomy department, Arctic-Lolcat Global Concept Limited cultivates high-yield staple crops and fresh vegetables using organic soil enrichment and solar-powered irrigation boreholes.

We also supply certified seeds, organic fertilizers, crop protection inputs, and agro-processing equipment (threshers, grinding machines, and farm tricycles) to empower farmers across Nigeria.

  • Cultivation of maize, cassava, tomatoes, okra, and leafy vegetables.
  • Supply of improved seeds, organic manure, fertilizers, and spraying pumps.
  • Solar-powered irrigation pumps, boreholes, threshers, and grinding machines.
